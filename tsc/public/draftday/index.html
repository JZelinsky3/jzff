How a pick goes

  1. The board puts a manager on the clock and the timer starts.
  2. He taps his player on Make Your Pick. The TV flips to THE PICK IS IN and gives nothing away.
  3. You hit Announce in 5s, walk to the TV and say the name. The board reveals him on cue with his card, his tags, his lineup and every time PAMS has drafted him before.
  4. He enters the pick in Sleeper. You hit Next Pick.

When you speed up

Rounds one through four are worth the ceremony. After that, flip the console to Live Feed and everyone just drafts in Sleeper as normal. The board keeps reading Sleeper every few seconds and fills the rail and the ticker on its own, with no buttons to press.

If something goes sideways

Any pick can be undone from the console log, which rewinds the clock to that pick. If a phone dies, use Enter a pick yourself. If the app falls over entirely, keep drafting in Sleeper and the board will catch up the moment it comes back.
