Plain-text alternative for launch-notice.html.

Paste this into the broadcast's text version in Resend rather than letting it
auto-generate one. An auto-generated text part from a table-heavy HTML email
comes out as a wall of link soup, and a mismatched or missing text part is one
of the cheapest spam signals to avoid. Keep the two versions saying the same
thing: filters compare them.

Resend merge tags work here too. Everything below the line is the body.
