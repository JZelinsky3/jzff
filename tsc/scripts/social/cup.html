How the Cup works
  1. 1

    The top 12 make it

    Seeded by your standings after Week 7. More than 12 teams, the top 12 get in. Fewer than 12, everyone does.

  2. 2

    Top seeds get byes

    Short of a full bracket? The best records skip round one.

  3. 3

    Your score is your Cup score

    One round a week. What you score in your league game counts, whoever you're playing.

  4. 4

    Win and move on

    Higher score advances. A tie goes to the higher seed.

  5. 5

    Pick the bracket

    Call every game before Thursday 8pm ET. Points for each right pick, more for the champion.
